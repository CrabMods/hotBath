# Growing bath herbs

Use the existing Bath Herb item on ordinary dirt, grass blocks, other dirt-tagged soil or farmland. No separate seed or recipe change is required; existing herbs and trades retain their item ID.

The crop has four growth stages. In sufficient light (level 9 or more), random ticks advance one stage with a one-in-five chance. Bone meal advances one or two stages. Growth stops at maturity.

Breaking a young crop returns one herb. A mature crop returns two, allowing one to be replanted and one to be used. Unsupported soil breaks the plant normally. The crop implements Minecraft's bone-meal interface and belongs to the crops block tag; accelerators that recognize those interfaces can discover it. Individual third-party growth machines have not all been tested.
