# Hot Bath 4.1.2

Bug fixes since 4.1.1 for Forge 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1.

- Fixed Hot Water Bucket heating recipes with Thirst Was Reclaimed and Polymorph. (#43, #49)
- Fixed missing localized bath fluid names. (#34)
- Fixed dirt overlay texture selection for some players and crouching alignment with Fresh Animations/EMF. (#72, #44)
- Fixed custom bath colors and opacity in Create tanks/spouts, and kept bath bottles upright on belts/depots. (#77, #80)
- Fixed Create honey, herbal and milk bath mixing ingredients on Forge. (#83)
- Preserved custom bath identity and fluid amounts through Create pipes and hose pulleys, including waterlogged blocks, incompatible drains and large fills. (#86, #89, #92, #94, #106)
- Fixed missed bath effects and extinguishing from short Create pipe bursts. (#100)
- Fixed stacked bath sources being overwritten and incomplete custom bath catalogs during synchronization. (#97, #103)
- Kept filled buckets when placement is rejected and respected Adventure waterlogging permissions on 1.21.1. (#109, #112)
